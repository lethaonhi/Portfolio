Why do I want to study in China?

China is Vietnam’s largest trading partner, particularly in agriculture. Through my research on the “volume trap” in Vietnamese agricultural exports, I became deeply aware of the structural challenges — declining unit values, weak brand equity, and limited value capture — that constrain Vietnam’s position in the Chinese market.

Studying in China will allow me to gain first-hand understanding of Chinese consumer markets, trade regulations, and value-chain dynamics. I hope to contribute to more strategic, higher-value cooperation between Vietnam and China in the years ahead.
